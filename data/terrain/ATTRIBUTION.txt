Mapzen Terrain Tiles. Global SRTM and GMTED2010 terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
USGS global elevation inputs are distributed as public-domain data; see source attribution for original distribution notices.

Modifications: bilinear sampling onto a 50m local grid over 10km x 10km around
36.5N, 127.8E. Godot subtracts the center reference elevation and flattens the
fictional base, with a 350m transition to the surrounding DEM. Horizontal and
vertical units are meters, with no vertical exaggeration. PBR ground materials
are illustrative; no satellite imagery, real buildings or land-cover survey
are included. These modifications are not endorsed by the source providers.
